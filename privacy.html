2. Information We Collect

Enquiry Details: Details you enter into our enquiry form, including your name, contact details (phone number, email), enquiry type (retail, wholesale, job work), selected products, and message.

Direct Communications: When you choose "Send on WhatsApp" or "Email instead", your enquiry details pre-fill into your chosen application. The website itself does not store or process this form data on internal web servers; data reaches us only when you choose to transmit the message through that application.

Technical & Log Data: Standard technical information received by our web hosting infrastructure and asset delivery providers (such as Google Fonts), including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages viewed.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data or financial credentials through this website.

3. How We Use Information

We process your information solely to:

  • Respond to your product and service enquiries.
  • Prepare and negotiate customised rate sheets, quotations, and contract terms for retail, bulk/wholesale, and job work orders.
  • Maintain website functionality, security, and integrity.
  • Comply with statutory legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Where data protection laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) or international frameworks (EU/UK GDPR) apply, we process information on the basis of your explicit consent (conferred when initiating contact), steps taken at your request prior to entering into a commercial agreement, or our legitimate business interest in addressing customer communications. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, nor do we disclose it for third-party behavioural advertising. Information is shared strictly with trusted service providers essential to operational communications (e.g., hosting providers, email servers, telecom/WhatsApp infrastructure) and authorised statutory or regulatory bodies when required by applicable law.

6. Third-Party Integrations

Fonts are loaded via Google Fonts.

Enquiries initiated through WhatsApp are processed by WhatsApp LLC / Meta in accordance with their independent terms and privacy notices.

7. Data Retention

We retain enquiry details only for the duration necessary to satisfy the commercial enquiry, manage recurring supplier/customer relationships, or comply with legal, tax, and accounting standards. After this operational period, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

8. Security

We maintain reasonable commercial, technical, and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, or unauthorised modification. However, no internet transmission or electronic data storage method is guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable.

9. Your Rights & Grievance Redressal

Under applicable privacy frameworks (including India's DPDPA, GDPR, and relevant consumer privacy regulations), you hold the right to access, rectify, update, or erase your personal information, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent.

Grievance Redressal: To exercise your rights or file a complaint regarding personal data, email our designated Grievance Officer at info@rasgruh.com. We will review and address enquiries within statutory timelines (typically 30 days).

If you reside in India and are dissatisfied with our response, you have the right to escalate unresolved grievances to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).

10. Cross-Border Transfers

Our cloud service and communication infrastructure providers may store or route data through servers situated outside your country of residence, under strict confidentiality and industry-standard security safeguards.

11. Minors

This website is intended for adult consumers and commercial trade buyers. We do not intentionally solicit or process personal information from individuals under 18 years of age.

12. Policy Modifications

We reserve the right to revise this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the active version.
